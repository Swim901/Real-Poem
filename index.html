Never Would've thought

By Swim Hubbard

If only I had known, tommorow would bring bittersweet happiness. Not told on my born day, I was left to find. No warning from cartoons, I thought they would love me like I do them. No signs from the stars, I thought they looked upon me like I did them. Not even Spider Man, I guess best friends are most quiet. Despair, Disgrace, Depression, Depreciation, and Disappointment; Forced to Decide and pushed to deliver. No one told me, I was left to find. And I made the discovery. Even the best of cakes are made with salt in them.
